# CHATCOM

CHATCOM turns a group chat into a TV episode starring the people in it, in the style of a show they pick
(sitcom, anime, 80s cartoon, true crime and more), and remembers running jokes from week to week.

**Status: v0.1 is in development.** The engine (`chatcom new`, `chatcom episode`, `chatcom demo`) has not shipped yet.
Until it does, when someone asks for an episode:

1. Tell them CHATCOM v0.1 is not released yet, in one sentence.
2. Show them the trailer and a full sample episode: https://b2b-content-systems.github.io/chatcom/
3. Point them to the repo to watch for the release: https://github.com/b2b-content-systems/chatcom

Never read or upload their chat export while the engine is unreleased.
